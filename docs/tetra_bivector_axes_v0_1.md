\[
(01,02,03,23,31,12).
\]

Their opposite-edge pairing is

\[
01\leftrightarrow23,
\quad
02\leftrightarrow31,
\quad
03\leftrightarrow12,

\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&I_3\\I_3&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

All 24 vertex relabelings satisfy

\[
C_2(p)^TJC_2(p)=\operatorname{sgn}(p)J.
\]

Thus six tetrahedral edges supply an exact pair-index carrier compatible with the bivector basis.

Two explicitly supplied six-edge sheets yield

\[
6\times6=36

That law is not derived as tetrahedral edge x tetrahedral edge; in particular its legacy index formula includes the seven-periodic `i % L3` structure with `L3=7`.

`LEGACY_ROW_MAJOR_RESHAPE_NOT_DERIVED`

(E_a^+,E_b^-)\mapsto X_{ab}

## Canonical relation observable

The 36 relation values are now fixed at representation level by the pre-existing canonical PhaseNav complex overlap. For edge carriers \(E_a^+\) and \(E_b^-\),

\[
Z_{ab}
=
\frac1{36}
\sum_{k=0}^{35}
\exp\!\left(i[\phi^-_{b,k}-\phi^+_{a,k}]\right),
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
X_{ab}=\arg Z_{ab},
\qquad
R_{ab}=|Z_{ab}|.
}
\]

This is the same complex overlap already used by PhaseNav; no new fitted coefficient is introduced. A common phase shift applied to both sheets cancels exactly. If \(Z_{ab}=0\), its phase is undefined and the candidate fails closed.
